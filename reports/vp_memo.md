\# Weekly Business Review — Olist Marketplace



\## Executive summary



The historical Olist marketplace dataset records \*\*R$13.22 million in delivered product revenue\*\* across 24 calendar months. Delivery performance stands out as a customer-experience concern: orders delivered late received an average review score of \*\*2.57/5\*\*, compared with \*\*4.29/5\*\* for early deliveries.



\## Performance scorecard



\- \*\*North Star:\*\* Delivered product revenue — R$13.22 million across the observed period.

\- \*\*Customer retention:\*\* Repeat-purchase rate of 3.12%, using unique customers with at least two distinct orders.

\- \*\*Delivery reliability:\*\* Approximately 8.11% of delivered orders with valid delivery-delay data arrived late.

\- \*\*Customer satisfaction:\*\* Average review score of 2.57/5 for late deliveries, versus 4.29/5 for early deliveries.



\## Key insight



Late delivery is associated with materially lower customer ratings. The 1.72-point difference between late and early deliveries suggests fulfilment reliability deserves focused investigation. This is an association, not proof of causation.



\## Recommended actions



1\. \*\*Investigate late-delivery drivers:\*\* Segment delays by seller, state, product category, and estimated shipping lead time.

2\. \*\*Prioritize high-risk orders:\*\* Monitor shipments approaching their estimated delivery dates and escalate likely delays.

3\. \*\*Improve repeat purchasing:\*\* Investigate the low observed repeat-purchase rate and test post-purchase retention initiatives.

4\. \*\*Review progress monthly:\*\* Track delivered product revenue alongside late-delivery rate and review score to avoid optimizing sales at the expense of customer experience.



\## Data and methodology notes



Revenue is the sum of item prices for delivered orders; freight is excluded. Repeat purchase uses `customer\_unique\_id` and counts distinct orders. Delivery analysis excludes delivered orders without a calculable delay. Historical results may not represent current marketplace conditions. The relationship between delivery timing and review scores is observational.
